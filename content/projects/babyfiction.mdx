Babyfiction is an e-commerce platform for fashion and accessories. The project connects the customer storefront with the tools needed to manage products, orders, promotions, and delivery.

## The shopping flow

The storefront includes a searchable product catalogue, a cart for guest and signed-in shoppers, a wishlist, and a checkout flow. Product reviews and ratings give each listing a place for customer feedback.

## Store operations

Inventory management, order handling, and promotions sit alongside a driver portal. The platform also includes newsletter subscriptions, sales analytics, and an AI customer-support chatbot.

## Services and stack

The Next.js and TypeScript frontend uses React, Radix UI, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui. An Express API with MongoDB and Mongoose supports the application.

The project also integrates JWT authentication, Cloudinary media management, Resend for email, SMS order notifications, and Google reCAPTCHA. The interface displays prices in South African rand and calculates VAT at 15%.
