Edu C2C Marketplace is a peer-to-peer shopping platform built with PHP and MySQL. It brings product discovery, account access, and marketplace administration into one web experience.

## Product discovery

Visitors can browse listings, search for products, and narrow results by category. Each listing has a detail view to support the next step from discovery to purchase.

## Marketplace administration

The admin dashboard brings listings, user accounts, and transactions into a single management area. Authentication protects account-specific parts of the platform.

## Built with

- **Application:** PHP
- **Data storage:** MySQL
- **Core areas:** product listings, search, category filters, authentication, and administration
